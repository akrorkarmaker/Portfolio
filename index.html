My story

Building for tomorrow.

I’m Akror Karmaker, a student and aspiring 3D Artist and VFX Artist from Bangladesh. I enjoy exploring visual storytelling, post-production and digital creation.

My current journey combines 3D work, VFX, video editing and foundational website development. I’m still learning and building my portfolio, and I believe consistent practice is the best way to improve.

Alongside creative work, I’m developing AmarAlaka — a community-focused project idea designed to make everyday local services easier to discover.
